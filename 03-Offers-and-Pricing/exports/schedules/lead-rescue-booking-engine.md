# Schedule A — Lead Rescue & Booking Engine™

> **DRAFT — requires review by a qualified attorney before client use.** Generated from the offer catalog.

## A1. Services
- Lead capture
- Missed-call recovery where technically supported and legally permitted
- Quote follow-up
- Appointment workflow
- CRM pipeline
- Lead reactivation with applicable consent
- Follow-up performance tracking

## A2. Fees
- One-time setup fee: $750
- Monthly service fee: $397
- Usage-based costs: SMS, calling minutes, phone numbers and carrier registration fees are usage-based and billed at cost. Estimated monthly usage is shown before go-live.
- Payment terms, minimum term and notice period: [OPEN — owner decision]

## A3. Scope limits
- Up to 3 inquiry sources
- Up to 2 follow-up sequences
- One booking calendar

## A4. Exclusions
- Usage-based messaging/calling costs (billed separately at cost)
- Live answering services
- Outreach to contacts without consent

## A5. Client responsibilities
- Phone provider details and delegated access
- Current CRM/booking tool access (delegated)
- Inquiry sources list
- Approved message wording
- Consent records for reactivation lists
- Grant access through delegated/user accounts. RRS will never ask for passwords in forms or email.

## A6. Reporting
- Inquiries by source
- Time to first response
- Follow-ups sent, replies, opt-outs
- Appointments booked
- Closed deals where the client documents them
- Usage costs

## A7. Compliance commitments
- TCPA and state telemarketing rules: obtain appropriate consent for automated texts/calls
- Honour opt-outs immediately; maintain suppression lists
- Clearly disclose usage-based messaging and calling costs
- Do not impersonate humans in automated messages

## A8. No guarantees
RRS does not guarantee search rankings, placement in AI-generated answers, review volumes, lead volumes or revenue. Reports describe observations and documented results only.

## A9. Cancellation and offboarding
1. Confirm cancellation in writing and the effective date per the agreement notice period.
2. Deliver a final report and export of client-owned data (CSV/JSON) and documents.
3. Transfer or confirm client ownership of every account, profile, domain and asset created.
4. Disable RRS automations and remove RRS from delegated access (GBP, analytics, CRM, website).
5. Record the offboarding in the client log; close open tasks; archive per data-retention policy.
6. Release or port phone numbers per client instruction; export consent and opt-out records.
