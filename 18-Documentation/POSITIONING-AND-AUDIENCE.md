# Positioning, Messaging & Audience Segmentation (draft)

## Positioning
**For** established local businesses in the Phoenix metro (Phoenix, Surprise, Peoria, Glendale, Scottsdale and nearby)
**who** lose customers before they ever talk to them, through weak Maps visibility, unclear AI and search presence, thin reviews, leaky websites and slow follow-up,
**Reale Revenue Systems** is a local revenue partner
**that** finds and fixes those leaks in one connected system,
**unlike** agencies that sell rankings or traffic in isolation and report vanity metrics.

**Line:** Get Discovered. Get Chosen. Get Booked.
**Supporting message:** "We help local businesses get found on Google and AI search tools, become the obvious choice, and turn more inquiries into paying customers."

## The Local Revenue System (five parts → three outcomes)
| Outcome | Part | Snapshot category | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Get Discovered | Maps & local search | Google Maps presence | A |
| Get Discovered | AI discovery | AI discovery readiness | B |
| Get Chosen | Reputation | Reviews & trust | C |
| Get Chosen | Website | Website conversion | E |
| Get Booked | Follow-up | Lead follow-up | D |

## Messaging pillars
1. **Evidence over promises.** We show what we checked, when, and what we couldn't verify.
2. **The whole path, not one channel.** Visibility without follow-up just wastes leads.
3. **Built for owners who are busy.** Short approvals, plain reports, phone-friendly.
4. **Honest growth.** No fake reviews, no doorway pages, no guaranteed rankings.
